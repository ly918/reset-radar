# Reset Radar v1.1.0 — English & 简体中文

English is now the default language. Switch instantly between **English** and **简体中文** in **Settings → Language**; the choice is saved on your Mac.

## What's new

- Localized main panel, settings, history, demo, menu bar descriptions, dates and error/status messages.
- New AI explanations follow the selected language. Posts and evidence stay verbatim; previously saved explanations retain their original language.
- Switching language does not invalidate a forecast or issue a paid API request.
- English README and updated screenshots, plus a Simplified Chinese README.
- Packaged localization validation and backward-compatible cached analysis metadata.

## Download

- `Reset-Radar-1.1.0-macOS-arm64.dmg`
- `Reset-Radar-1.1.0-macOS-arm64.dmg.sha256`

For **Apple Silicon, macOS 14+**. Open the DMG and drag the app into Applications, replacing the previous version. Existing settings, Keychain credentials and cached data are retained. macOS may request Keychain authorization for the updated app.

The app uses ad-hoc signing and is not Apple notarized. Probabilities remain experimental and uncalibrated; they do not predict individual account resets. No Windows, Intel or iOS installer is included.

## 中文

默认界面改为英文，在 **Settings → Language** 中可即时切换简体中文并保存选择。新 AI 解释遵循所选语言，原帖及证据保持原文；切换语言不会自动重跑分析或消耗额度。旧版配置和缓存可继续使用。
